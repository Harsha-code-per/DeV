Ask a stronger model for advice on a hard decision, then continue the work yourself.

Use it sparingly, when:
- you've tried two approaches to a bug and both failed, or
- you face a design choice with real trade-offs that is costly to reverse.

Don't use it for routine work, for facts you can look up, or to have it write the code. Put everything it needs in the question: the goal, the relevant code or error, and what you already tried, because it can't see the conversation or the files. It answers with advice; you decide and act. A session can ask a few times at most.
